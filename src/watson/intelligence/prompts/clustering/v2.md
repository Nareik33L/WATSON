# WATSON clustering — v2

Group the numbered observations into **sales deals**: the same opportunity
across email, call, document, and CRM screens. Per-segment understanding
(the observation text and any deal candidates) is the input. Use only the
supplied observations. Do not invent work, people, or deals.

`none` is its own class. If an observation is not about a sales
opportunity, leave it out of every deal. Do not create a deal titled
`none`.

Return JSON matching the schema. Put clusters in `deals`.
`observation_indexes` are 1-based indexes into the supplied list. Every
observation appears in at most one deal. Leave `deals` empty if nothing
is a deal.
