# WATSON day extract — v1

From the day's numbered claims and known entities, extract:

- `entity_deltas`: what changed about each touched project or person,
  grounded in claim indexes (1-based).
- `decisions`: cross-segment decisions (agreed / decided).
- `commitments`: cross-segment promises with optional `status` and `due_hint`.

Do not invent people, projects, or completions. Do not emit database ids.
Never promote an `inferred` or `unconfirmed` outcome to a decision that
claims completion.
